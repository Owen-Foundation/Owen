# Code of Conduct — Owen 2

Owen 2 is **GPL-3.0** by [Owen Foundation](https://github.com/Owen-Foundation).

We follow the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or experience level.

## Standards

- Be welcoming and respectful.
- Be collaborative — focus on what is best for the community.
- Do not submit copied code from other engines.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers at **Owen Foundation**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated.
